Funds bridged mid-trace: how do you keep the evidence chain intact for court?

InvestigationSolved#bridges#evidence#INV-201·11 replies·684 views·Watched by 46 members

We are tracing investment-fraud proceeds: victim funds were swapped into a stablecoin, moved through four wallets and bridged to a second network within 40 minutes. In an earlier case defence counsel called the bridge link “inference, not evidence”.

How are you documenting the bridge step so it holds up in court, and what do you request from the bridge operator first?

Elena PetrovaCorvennaInstructor · INV-201 17 Reply#2 · 19 Nov, 17:48
Lead Blockchain Investigator · Corvenna Forensics

Treat the bridge as two transactions you can prove and one link you must explain.

  1. Capture the lock on the source network and the release on the destination: block, time and amount, from two sources.
  2. Match the two events on the bridge’s own deposit ID, not on amount and time. Amount-and-time is exactly what gets challenged.
  3. Write the match up as a one-page methodology note: fields matched, tolerance, who reviewed it.

Same day, send the operator a preservation request: deposit record, relayer logs and any customer data.

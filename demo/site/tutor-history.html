What is the practical difference between a reserve attestation and a full audit? Which one should our risk committee rely on?
Chronopoli AI Tutor · 20:12
An attestation is a point-in-time opinion on whether stated reserves existed and matched circulating supply on a specific date. A full audit covers a period, tests controls and gives an opinion on the financial statements as a whole.For a risk committee, treat attestations as necessary but not sufficient: ask for the frequency, the reserve composition by liquidity tier and any qualifications in the accountant's report.

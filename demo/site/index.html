How to use the demo

Keep the unzipped folder together and open index.html in any current web browser. Nothing needs to be installed. The windows are designed for a 1600 px wide screen; on a smaller screen they are scaled to fit.

1Start

Press Start the tour or open any window below. Then click around as in a real product: the top menu (Dashboard, Courses, Districts, Community, Video Library) and the account menu at the top right all lead somewhere.

2Navigator

The bar at the bottom shows window n / 58, the menu path and title, and who is signed in. Prev and Next follow the story; Map lists every window. Collapse it to a small pill with the minus button.

‹ Prev 12 / 58 Courses › Course page Next › ☰ Map

3Keyboard

← →previous / next window Mopen the map of all windows Hhighlight what you can click Ffit windows to the browser width (on by default) Escclose the map

4AI Tutor

Open the gold AI Tutor button, type a question and press Enter. The answer streams in with its sources, offline, from a small sample of course material. Try “How should I size an intraday liquidity buffer?”

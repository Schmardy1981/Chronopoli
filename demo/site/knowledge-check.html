Correct · 1 of 1 point

Size for the window, not the day: 40% of the Thursday volume lands between 10:00 and 13:00. Then add headroom for a congested network. The 180,000 above the operating tranche becomes the shared reserve you can move to either network within one rebalancing cycle.

1,800,000 × 40% = 720,000  →  720,000 × 1.25 = 900,000
